FAQ

Simple by design.

Does this replace accounting advice?

No. These are planning and pricing tools based on the numbers you enter. Tax and accounting treatment varies by business and location.

Are the numbers uploaded?

No. The free calculators run entirely in your browser. The current MVP does not send pricing inputs to a server.

Why price from margin instead of adding a percentage?

Markup is measured against cost while margin is measured against the selling price. If you need a specific margin, the correct formula solves backward from the final price.
